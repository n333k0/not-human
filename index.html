How it works

From the audit to agents in production

/01 Audit

AI opportunity audit · 1–2 weeks

We map how work really moves: interviews with the people doing it, a read-only look at your systems, every spreadsheet on the side. You get a costed map and the first workflow ranked by payback.

See a case study

/02 Architecture

Process architecture & redesign

Every step gets the cheapest owner that works: delete it, write a rule, give it to an agent, or keep a person on it. Then we design how agents, systems and people connect.

/03 Deployment

Production deployment · first workflow in 3–4 weeks

Agents go live inside the software you already run, each with its own login and only the keys its job needs, on a slice of real work first.

/04 Optimisation

Agent system optimisation · every month

We strengthen each agent's decisions, extend it into new work and report against the audit's number at 30, 90 and 180 days. What doesn't pay is switched off.

/05 Your process

30 minutes · no prep

Show us the workflow that eats the most hours. You leave with where it leaks and one first step.

Questions

What companies ask first
What happens on the first call?

Thirty minutes on video, no prep. We go through how the workflow you care about runs today, where an agent could realistically take work over, which systems it would need and what a timeline looks like. You leave with one clear first step.

How long until an agent is in production?

The audit takes 1–2 weeks. The first workflow usually goes live 3–4 weeks into the build, on a slice of real work first.

What can agents actually do?

Read and match documents, watch your systems, draft replies and proposals, route approvals, update records and assemble reports, across the tools that used to need someone copying between them. Money, commitments and anything irreversible stay with a named person.

How much of our team's time does it take?

One process owner who can make decisions, some time with the people who do the work, and read-only access to the main systems. We do the mapping, writing, building and deployment.

Can it be built around our own workflows?

That is the only way we build. Every system starts from the map of how your team actually does the work, with your rules and your exceptions.

Do we have to change our software?

No. Agents connect to the CRM, accounting, inbox, chat and databases you already run. No migration.

How do you handle security?

Each agent has its own account and only the permissions its job needs. Every action is logged and every agent has an off switch. Money and commitments stay with a named person.

How is it priced?

It depends on the workflow, the systems and the scope. The audit is a fixed fee credited in full to the build, and the build is quoted once the map is done.
